Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my size?

Choose from XS to XL (waist sizes 28 to 36 for jeans). If you are between sizes, we suggest sizing up for a relaxed fit. Contact us for help.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timelines depend on your location. Add your real delivery details to this answer before launching.

Can I exchange an item?

Our policy allows a simple size exchange within 7 days of delivery for unworn items with tags.

Is shipping free?

Yes, shipping is free on orders above ₹1,999. Otherwise a flat ₹99 fee applies.

How do I use a coupon?

Enter your code in the coupon box on the shopping bag page. Try VELORA10 for 10% off in this demo.

Is checkout real?

No. This is a demo storefront: checkout shows an order confirmation but no payment is taken and no order is placed.
